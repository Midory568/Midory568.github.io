Osaka大阪

大阪Osaka

Dotonbori is busy after dark, with large signs above the canal and queues outside the food stalls.

入夜后的道顿堀很热闹，河边挂着巨大的招牌，小吃摊前排着队。

City Impression城市印象

The shopping streets stay busy late into the evening. Away from them, the residential streets are quieter.

商业街到了晚上仍然很热闹，拐进附近的居民区就安静多了。

A Day in Osaka一日随记

  • Morning上午Begin with Osaka Castle and its surrounding park.从大阪城及周边公园开始。
  • Afternoon下午Walk around Namba and the covered shopping streets.逛逛难波和有顶棚的商业街。
  • Evening晚上Walk along the Dotonbori canal and try local street food.沿道顿堀运河散步并品尝当地小吃。

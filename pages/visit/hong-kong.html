Travel journal · August 23–29

Hong Kong

A week in Hong Kong.

I spent a week in Hong Kong, from August 23 to 29. What stayed with me most was the sense of freedom. I could walk into a bookstore and find books and perspectives I rarely came across back home. Even something as simple as browsing the shelves felt new to me. Here are some photos and stories from those seven days.

01 · Harbour nights 02 · Campus visits 03 · Everyday discoveries

01 / By the water

Hong Kong after dark

I took this photo by the harbour at night. The Observation Wheel was glowing red, and the buildings behind it were still lit up. This is how I remember the waterfront after dark.

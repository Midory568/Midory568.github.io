Sapporo札幌

札幌Sapporo

In winter, snow covers Sapporo's wide streets. The straight roads and regular blocks make the city easy to navigate.

冬天的札幌，宽阔的街道上铺着积雪。道路笔直，街区方正，认路并不难。

City Impression城市印象

The underground passages offer a warm break from the snowy streets. Above ground, light from shop windows falls onto the snow.

外面冷的时候，可以到地下通道里暖和一下。地面上，沿街店铺的灯光照在雪地里。

A Day in Sapporo一日随记

  • Morning上午Walk through the snow near Odori.在大通附近的雪中散步。
  • Afternoon下午Pause for coffee and a warm bowl of ramen.喝杯咖啡，再吃一碗热拉面。
  • Evening晚上See the winter lights across the city centre.欣赏市中心的冬季灯光。

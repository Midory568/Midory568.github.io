札幌Sapporo

Sapporo in winter feels spacious and quiet. Snow softens the wide streets while the grid of the city remains clear beneath the changing weather.

冬季的札幌宽阔而安静。积雪柔化了宽广的街道，而城市清晰的网格结构仍然透过不断变化的天气显现出来。

City Impression城市印象

Fresh snow, warm underground passages, broad avenues, and softly lit windows create a calm northern atmosphere.

新雪、温暖的地下通道、宽阔大道和柔和灯光下的窗户，共同构成平静的北方氛围。

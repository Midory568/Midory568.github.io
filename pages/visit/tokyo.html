东京Tokyo

These notes focus on Shibuya, from the familiar crossing to the manga and game shops in the surrounding streets.

这页主要记下涩谷的几处地方：熟悉的十字路口，还有周围街道上的漫画店和游戏店。

Anime动漫

Shibuya & Anime涩谷与动漫

The crossing, elevated railway, and crowded side streets make Shibuya easy to recognise in anime. The area also has character shops, game stores, and temporary exhibitions.

十字路口、高架铁路和拥挤的小街，让动漫里的涩谷很容易辨认。附近也有动漫周边店、游戏店和限时展览。

Shibuya Impression涩谷印象

At the crossing, screens change overhead while crowds pass in every direction. In the side streets, music from the shops mixes with the street noise.

路口上方的屏幕不停切换，人群从各个方向穿过。走进旁边的小街，还能听见店里放的音乐。

A Day in Shibuya涩谷一日

  • Morning上午Take photos at the crossing, then walk through the side streets.在十字路口拍些照片，再逛逛旁边的小街。
  • Afternoon下午Browse manga and game shops, or stop at a temporary exhibition.逛逛漫画店、游戏店，或者看看限时展览。
  • Evening晚上Walk around the crossing after the signs light up.等招牌亮起后，在路口附近走走。

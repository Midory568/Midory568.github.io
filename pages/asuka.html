Neon Genesis Evangelion

Soryu Asuka Langley

惣流·明日香·兰格雷

Asuka is the Second Child and the pilot of Evangelion Unit-02. Brilliant, forceful, and intensely competitive, she treats piloting as proof that she deserves to be recognised.

Her bold confidence sits beside a profound fear of being unwanted or replaceable. That contrast gives her story its emotional force: Asuka is not only a gifted pilot, but also a young person learning how to live with vulnerability.

About Asuka

Confidence, Pride, and Vulnerability

Evangelion Unit-02

Asuka pilots Unit-02 with extraordinary technical skill and a fierce belief that she should be the best. The red Evangelion becomes an extension of her determination and identity.

A Character in Tension

Her directness and bravado are both real and protective. The series lets her be difficult, proud, funny, hurt, and resilient without reducing her to any one of those qualities.

About Eva

Neon Genesis Evangelion

Neon Genesis Evangelion is a landmark 1995 anime created by Hideaki Anno. In a post-apocalyptic Tokyo-3, teenagers are asked to pilot biomechanical Evangelions to defend humanity against mysterious beings known as Angels.

Beyond its striking action and design, the series explores loneliness, identity, family, and the difficult desire to connect with others. Its blend of intimate character drama, religious imagery, and psychological science fiction has made it one of anime's most influential works.

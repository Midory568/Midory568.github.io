Soryu Asuka Langley

惣流·明日香·兰格雷

Asuka pilots Evangelion Unit-02 in Neon Genesis Evangelion. She is outspoken and competitive, and takes pride in being a pilot.

She wants to be recognised for what she can do, but struggles when she feels unwanted. Her arguments and moments of doubt are as much a part of her story as the battles.

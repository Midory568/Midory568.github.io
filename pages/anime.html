Character spotlight

Rikka Takanashi

小鳥遊六花 · Takanashi Rikka

Rikka is the central heroine of Love, Chunibyo & Other Delusions!. She calls herself the wielder of the “Tyrant's Eye,” treating ordinary school life as part of an elaborate battle against unseen forces. Her eyepatch, dramatic gestures, and made-up spells are not merely a joke: they become a way for her to hold onto a private world when reality feels too difficult to face.

Behind the fantasy language is someone quiet, earnest, and deeply attached to the people who accept her. Her growing bond with Yuuta turns the series into a gentle story about grief, imagination, and learning that being understood does not require abandoning the things that once helped you cope.

Weathering with You

In a rain-soaked Tokyo, runaway student Hodaka meets Hina, a girl who can briefly clear the sky. Their small weather service brings sunlight to other people, but the miracle carries a cost and forces them to choose between the expectations of the world and the person they want to protect.

TV series · 2012

The Pet Girl of Sakurasou

さくら荘のペットな彼女

Sorata lives among the eccentric residents of Sakura Hall and is asked to look after Mashiro, a brilliant artist who struggles with ordinary routines. Beneath its romantic comedy, the series is honest about talent, effort, failure, jealousy, and learning to keep creating beside gifted friends.

TV series · 2012

Love, Chunibyo & Other Delusions!

中二病でも恋がしたい！

Yuuta wants to leave his embarrassing “Dark Flame Master” past behind, but Rikka still sees secret powers and fantasy battles everywhere. Their relationship turns adolescent imagination into something tender: not simply a phase to mock, but a language for loneliness, grief, and affection.

TV series · 2011

Guilty Crown

ギルティクラウン

In a near-future Tokyo ruled after a devastating outbreak, Shu Ouma gains the power to draw weapons called Voids from other people. His encounter with the singer Inori pulls him into a resistance movement, where power, trust, and sacrifice become impossible to separate.

TV series · 2020

Jujutsu Kaisen

After swallowing one of Sukuna's cursed fingers to save his friends, Yuji Itadori enters the world of jujutsu sorcerers. The series combines kinetic battles with a sharp focus on what people choose to protect when fear, loss, and duty collide.
